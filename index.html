Our Story

A Kitchen Born from the Heart of Uganda

"Every plate we serve carries the memory of a grandmother's kitchen, a village fire, and the warmth of Ugandan hospitality."

Savanna Grill & Kitchen was born on Hoima Road in Nansana out of a deep love for authentic Ugandan flavours. From slow-cooked Luwombo wrapped in banana leaves to freshly grilled Nile Tilapia, everything on our menu is a tribute to the rich culinary heritage of Uganda.

We source every ingredient locally - from the freshest matoke in the market to the finest groundnut paste - ensuring that what reaches your table is not just food, but an experience.
